## Priority evidence gaps

1. Practices: obtain 1998-era Sun Italia material, internal practice maps, decision rights, operational records and independent former-employee accounts.
2. Buurtzorg: distinguish team-level autonomy from central support and external health-system effects.
3. Morning Star: obtain the full case and independent data where available; do not treat teaching material as a causal study.
4. FAVI and Sun Hydraulics: distinguish organizational narratives from measured longitudinal outcomes.
5. Zappos: separate the Holacracy design, implementation process, employee departures and subsequent organizational changes.
6. Cross-case: collect comparable unit-level measures before attempting pooled statistics.
