# Weighted combinatorial Hodge Laplacians on Q6

Finite-dimensional construction only. Positive diagonal inner products are assigned to 0-, 1- and 2-cochains. The resulting symmetric Gram operators are positive semidefinite. Their harmonic dimensions reproduce the Q6 cellular Betti numbers (1,0,111). This does not define a metric-derived dual-cell Hodge star or establish a continuum or physical limit.
