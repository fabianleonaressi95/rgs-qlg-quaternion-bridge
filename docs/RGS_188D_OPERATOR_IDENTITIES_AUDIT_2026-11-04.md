# RGS 188D operator identities: audit record (2026-11-04)

This record reports the finite matrix checks from the integrated audit artifact generated by GitHub Actions run 41803424888 (PR #62, commit e9bd7480e2935fc8736deffe93db588aa9d0ac6b). It is not a claim that the original 192-dimensional energy Hessian has been recovered.

## Results

### Original 192x192 Hessian

The audited `data/rgs_188d` inventory contained 66 NumPy files/arrays and no array with shape (192,192). The repository-wide scan added in the current integrated audit now searches both .npy files and every array inside .npz archives. Until that scan produces a source-backed candidate and its construction/provenance is recovered, the original Hessian remains **NOT RECOVERED**. A shape match alone would only make an array a candidate Hessian.

For `K0.npy` and `K0_inv.npy`, the relative Frobenius difference is \\(9.5241349474\\times10^{-14}\\): they are numerically the same matrix, not a matrix and its inverse. Their product is not close to identity.

For `K_tangent_rebuilt.npy` and `Kinv_rebuilt.npy`, the product has rank and trace 185. The identity residual \\(\\sqrt{3}\\) is consistent with a rank-185 orthogonal projector in a 188-dimensional space, not with a failed ordinary inverse. All four Moore–Penrose residuals pass:
- relative \\(\\|ABA-A\\|_F/\\|A\\|_F=5.5086654713\\times10^{-15}\\);
- relative \\(\\|BAB-B\\|_F/\\|B\\|_F=3.6314754481\\times10^{-13}\\);
- \\(\\|(AB)^T-AB\\|_F=8.4064019115\\times10^{-13}\\);
- \\(\\|(BA)^T-BA\\|_F=8.3603693498\\times10^{-13}\\).

Thus the rebuilt pair is verified as mutual Moore–Penrose pseudoinverses at the stated numerical tolerance. The three-dimensional nullspace is consistent with the product projector; its physical interpretation still depends on the operator definition and boundary/constraint conventions.

## Interpretation and next gates

1. Do not call `K0_inv` an inverse of `K0`; the archived pair is numerically near-identical and fails the product test.
2. Treat `Kinv_rebuilt` as the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse of `K_tangent_rebuilt`, not as an ordinary inverse.
3. Keep `K0 (1)`/`Kinv` separate until their provenance and operator definitions are established.
4. Recover the missing 192x192 Hessian from its generating notebook/source, including constraints, units, coordinate order and Hessian convention. No synthetic substitute is admissible as the original.
5. The T2u triplet reconstruction is numerically verified against the archive, but a full source-provenance chain for every upstream input is still required.

All residuals above are finite numerical evidence. They do not establish a continuum limit, a physical interpretation of the nullspace, or a derivation of TEGR/Dirac dynamics.
