# Algebraic Einstein–Cartan axial-torsion benchmark (Phase 1)

## Purpose and scope

This module establishes a reproducible interface between a fermionic axial-current
input and an **algebraic** totally antisymmetric torsion response. It is not a
complete Einstein–Cartan–Dirac theory.

In minimal Einstein–Cartan theory, varying the independent connection gives an
algebraic Cartan equation when no torsion kinetic term is added. Eliminating that
torsion yields a local spin–spin interaction. Propagating torsion requires extra
dynamical terms and is not implied by minimal EC. TEGR torsion is the torsion of
a teleparallel connection and cannot be identified with EC contorsion without
an explicit connection map.

## Explicit parameterized relation

The implementation uses eta=diag(-1,+1,+1,+1) and a contravariant coordinate
Levi-Civita symbol with epsilon[0,1,2,3]=+1. It evaluates

    T_abc = c_T * kappa * epsilon_abcd * J5^d.

The coefficient c_T is explicit, not hidden. Its default -1/4 is a plumbing-test
placeholder only. Its sign and normalization must be derived from the exact
EC-Dirac action, gamma-matrix conventions, spin-current definition and epsilon
orientation before physical interpretation. Missing convention or units IDs
are rejected.

The module checks total antisymmetry and the residual of this parameterized
relation, and computes T_abc T^abc using the stated Minkowski metric. It does
not infer an effective four-fermion coefficient.

## RGS–PSU integration contract

A future adapter may accept a fermionic axial current only after it is derived
from an explicit spinor field and action. The Q-ball U(1) Noether charge is not
a spin density and must not be passed as J5^a. The TorsionLambda cosmological
ansatz H^2 proportional to rho + beta*rho_spin remains phenomenological until
its spin density, dimensions, averaging prescription and conservation law are
derived.

A bridge to RGS teleparallel variables must map connections (and therefore
torsion/contorsion conventions), not merely match tensor names.

## Evidence status

- **PROVED (software identity):** the tensor generated by the model relation is
  totally antisymmetric because it is proportional to the Levi-Civita symbol.
- **NUMERICALLY VERIFIED:** tests check linearity, parameterized Cartan residual,
  finite quadratic invariant and rejection of missing metadata.
- **CANDIDATE:** a convention-explicit interface for later EC-Dirac matching.
- **OPEN:** derive c_T and the induced four-fermion term by varying one fully
  specified EC-Dirac action; derive spin current from spinors; construct
  cosmological averaging and GR limit; only then compare with TorsionLambda
  and map to RGS/TEGR.
