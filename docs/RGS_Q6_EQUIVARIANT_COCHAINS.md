# Equivariant cubical cochains on Q6

The finite cubical complex has 64 vertices, 192 canonically oriented edges
and 240 elementary square faces. The coboundaries are
(D_0:C^0\to C^1) and (D_1:C^1\to C^2), with exact
(D_1D_0=0). The square orientation is
(v\to v\oplus e_i\to v\oplus e_i\oplus e_j\to v\oplus e_j\to v).

Each of the 60 proper icosahedral rotations acts on Q6 vertices by the
signed-axis action from the intertwiner. Its induced actions on edges and
faces include orientation signs. The audit verifies orthogonality and
(D_0P_0=P_1D_0), (D_1P_1=P_2D_1).

This is a finite equivariant cochain complex, not a spin structure, Clifford
module, Lorentz connection, fermion propagator or physical Dirac operator.
