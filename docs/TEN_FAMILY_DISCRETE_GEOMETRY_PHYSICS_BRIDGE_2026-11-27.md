[Ten-family discrete differential geometry to physics bridge](https://github.com/fabianleonaressi95/theorem/blob/rgs-q6-antitwin-spacetime-bridge/research/TEN_FAMILY_DISCRETE_DIFFERENTIAL_GEOMETRY_PHYSICS_BRIDGE_2026-11-27.md).

Eight shared discrete-geometry interfaces are represented as separate nodes: oriented cellular complex, metric Hodge structure, gauge transport, discrete coframe, independent Cartan connection, spinorial lift, variational field system, and refinement/continuum comparison.

Sixteen record-to-family links were added only after inspecting the corresponding theorem cards (T027, T048, T049D, T115, T129, T130, T146–T148 and T161). They identify the mathematical object contributed by each record; they are not proof-dependency arrows. Family-to-interface links are architecture relations, not theorem implications.

The graph JSON is schema v5, currently 411 nodes and 780 edges. The earlier 306 register-derived arrows remain explicitly unreviewed. The ten-family bridge does not upgrade any theorem status or claim a completed physical model.

Key safeguards:
- Q6 graph beta_1 is not the first Betti number of its filled cubical 2-skeleton.
- The six-dimensional golden Euclidean split does not select Lorentzian time.
- IVM cubic O_h and golden icosahedral I_h are not identified without a constructed equivariant metric map.
- Anti-Twin sheet exchange is not Cartan torsion.
- Generic Riemann–Cartan geometry, torsion-free geometry and teleparallel R=0 geometry are distinct branches.
- T115 proves a Clifford identity for a prescribed coframe; it does not derive a connection or field equations.
- T149's bare Q6 Clifford operator remains a spectral obstruction, not a continuum Dirac operator.
- Mosco convergence requires an actual refinement sequence, Hilbert-space comparison maps, and the relevant liminf/recovery and compactness arguments.
